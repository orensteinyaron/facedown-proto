The insight

Don't fight the phone.
Make presence the game.

  • You cannot force people off their phones. Willpower apps and lectures fail.
  • So flip it: the app becomes the cage. Everyone keeps it open, phones face-down. The moment you crack, you are out.
  • The tension of who breaks first is the thrill. No casino, no gimmick.
  • Detection is honest and presence-only: the app is open or it is not. We never watch your screen.

How it is played, the core night

Stake in.
Last one standing takes the pot.

  • Four friends go out. One opens a Table. Everyone stakes Aura, the in-app currency, into a shared pot.
  • Phones face-down, app open. Crack and pick up your phone, you are out, and your stake stays in the pot.
  • The pot grows as the table thins. The last one standing takes the whole pot.

The engine

Every table is an install.

Growth is not ads. It is the game itself. One person starts a Table, and the crew joins by scanning a QR code, right there in the room.
01 · in the room
Scan the QR

The host opens a Table. Everyone points their camera at the code.

02 · both paths
App or no app

Have the app: you are in instantly. No app: a fast download and a roughly 30 second onboarding.

03 · no dead end
Same live table

Everyone lands in the exact same table, at peak motivation: friends waiting, pot live, pressure on.

  • Two social forces do the work: friends pull you in (you cannot be the one who won't play), then pressure you to win (don't be the one who cracks).
  • It travels beyond the room too: the trophy card becomes a "beat our 2h14" callout that reaches people who were not there, and it is TikTok native.
Net effect: near zero customer acquisition cost. The product is its own distribution.

Why they come back for months

One night is a thrill.
The habit is the season.

Retention is not in the session. It is a status you carry between nights, the meta-game around the match, like Fortnite, Duolingo, and Snapchat.
🔥 recruit

Crew Streak

A shared streak. Letting it die betrays your friends, so it drags them back out. Streaks are proven habit engines: about 35% of Snapchat's 13 to 24 daily users keep at least one going.

market.us/statistics/social-media/snapchat
🏆 dominate

Aura League

A local season ladder against your own crew, always "200 from #1." At Duolingo, leagues and streaks cut churn from 47% to 28%, and leaderboard-active users do 40% more per week.

The Flex

Scarce, ranked, season-only status worth bragging about. Grace is built in: a lost round still feeds your season, so failure feeds the loop instead of ending it.

Theater / B2B2C
Theaters already ask. We make it a game.
  • Theaters, cinemas, and venues already ask every audience to put phones away. Today that ask is passive and easy to ignore.
  • Facedown turns it into a shared event: a QR on the screen, the whole audience scans and joins the face-down challenge, and newcomers onboard in seconds.
  • At the end, recognition from the venue, or an optional sponsor perk delivered by a scan (a free drink, a discount, merch). The sponsor is the bonus, not the point.
  • Every show onboards a whole audience at once. Spectacle and top-of-funnel in one.

Aura, the currency

Earn it by being present.
Spend it on status.

  • Aura is the in-app currency. You earn it by being present, and you can buy more.
  • Stake it into a table's pot, unlock rare reveal skins and cosmetics, buy streak insurance so one bad night does not break your run, and enter premium or sponsored seasons.
  • One clean line for a gaming investor: Aura is buyable but not cashable. There is no cash-out, so it is a cosmetic and status economy (the Slotomania and Zynga Poker model), not gambling.

The free model

Free to start, always.

The rule · always true

Never kill the viral moment

The first table you are invited to is always free. The instant a friend pulls you in, you play. No wall, no waitlist. That is what protects the near-zero-CAC loop.
