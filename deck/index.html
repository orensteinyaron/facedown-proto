What we found

Don't fight the phone.
Make presence the game.

  • You can't force people off their phones — willpower apps and lectures don't work.
  • So flip it: the app becomes the cage. Everyone keeps it open, phones face-down. The second you crack, you're out.
  • The uncertainty of who breaks first is the thrill. No casino, no gimmick — the room itself is the slot machine.

Built to last, and to defend

The credibility spine.

Honest referee

App-foreground detection — reliable, cross-platform, no phone-surveillance. It's a game, not spyware.

Grace built in

A lost round still feeds your season — failure feeds the loop instead of ending it. Our anti-BeReal design.

Legal firewall

Aura coins are buyable but never cashable — the cash-out door is never built. That one cut wire keeps us in the safe lane (the Slotomania / Zynga model) while cash-redeemable coins get criminalized around us.
